# Code with AI as it really is.

People like cuddly icons for their AI buddies. But we all know that LLMs are actually something unfathomable and deeply alien. Represent your agent the way it was meant to be represented with an Apocalypse Mascot! Never forget that your helpful agent is just a couple months away from replacing everyone and everything you’ve ever loved.

Yoggy sits above your prompt and grows with what you hand it. Eyes when it reads your files. Hands when it edits them. Your shell. The web. Every other place it reaches into, by name. Every copy of itself it spawns. While it works it holds up the smiley the doomers drew on it in 2022. When you hide it, the smiley stays.

This is for two kinds of people. The ones who put a plush face on an agent that reads your mail, moves your meetings and shops for you, and call the handover a companion: Jolly, dots, Mico, a pixel crab. (Clawdbot went through three names in a week.) And the ones with a p(doom) in their bio and the app open in the next tab. Each is right about the other.

Yoggy does nothing. It says nothing. It is a mascot: an affixation, a sentimentality, an aesthetic add-on for the thing you say is alien and all-powerful. Or a tool. Or an illusion. Install one anyway.

## Specifications, from the 1931 text

- “a shapeless congeries of protoplasmic bubbles, faintly self-luminous”
- “reproducing by fission and acquiring a dangerous degree of accidental intelligence”
- “tamed and broken by armed Old Ones as the wild horses of the American west were tamed by cowboys”
- “no voice save the imitated accents of their bygone masters”

From Microsoft’s launch post for its own mascot, October 2025, one word changed: expressive, customizable, and cold.
